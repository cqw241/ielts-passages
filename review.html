How review works

Recognition: understand words in context. Production: retrieve words and use them in new sentences. Each skill has its own schedule.

Active reviews both skills; Recognition reviews reading only. Already know and Skip pause review.

Rate each attempt with Again, Hard, Good or Easy. Up to 6 new words start each day; new words pause at 20 due tasks. Production waits 24 hours after an answer is shown.

Established: two successful reviews in a row and a 14+ day interval. Production also needs an independent sentence after the latest Again.
