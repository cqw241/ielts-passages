How review works

Recognition: understand words in context. Production: retrieve words and use them in new sentences. Each skill has its own schedule.

New saved or manually added words default to Active, which reviews both skills. Add a meaning before review can start. Recognition reviews reading only; Already know and Skip pause review. Existing learning goals stay unchanged.

Rate each attempt with Again, Hard, Good or Easy. You can save any number of words; up to 6 new words start each local calendar day. New-word admission reserves 2 task slots per word within a 20-task capacity. At 19 backlog tasks, only 1 slot remains, so new words wait; at 20, no slots remain. Production waits 24 hours after an answer is shown.

Established: two successful reviews in a row and a 14+ day interval. Production also needs an independent sentence after the latest Again. A word is established when every enrolled track meets these criteria.
