How your two review tracks work

Recognition: understand a word in its article context. Production: retrieve an expression, complete a new context, rewrite an idea, then write your own sentence. Each track has its own schedule. Targets stay hidden in the task queue. When both tracks are ready, Production comes first. After a target or reference is shown, Production waits at least 24 hours before another independent attempt, even if you finish without rating. This separation survives reloading and backup.

Again retries Recognition after 10 minutes and independent Production after at least 1 day. Good progresses through 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days. Hard shortens the interval; Easy advances faster. Performance adjusts intervals, up to 120 days. Successful late reviews receive a small interval adjustment.

Up to 6 new words start each day. New words pause when 20 review tasks are due. Active words train both tracks; Recognition words train reading only. Already know and Skip pause practice without claiming tested mastery.

Mastery is a self-assessed indicator: at least two consecutive successful attempts and an interval of 14 days or more. Production also needs a successful independent sentence after the latest Again. Delayed recall reports attempts separated by at least 7 days. Late recall reports attempts at least 1 day overdue.
